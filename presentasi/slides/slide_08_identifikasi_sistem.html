*Data riil uji boks miniatur: y₀ = 2800 ADC, y_ss = 1600 ADC, Δu = 200 PWM, sampling 50ms.

2. Transport Delay (L)
L = 1.5 detik

3. Konstanta Waktu (τ — Aturan 63.2%)
y(L+τ) = 2800 - (0.632 × 1200) = 2041.6 → τ = 7.0 detik
Inersia kapasitas volume udara boks mencapai 63.2% perubahan total.
